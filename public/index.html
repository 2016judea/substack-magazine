Fills the front cover edge to edge. Portrait images work best; 2550 × 3300 px or larger prints sharp.

  1. Go to mixam.com/magazines and scroll to Create Your Magazines.
  2. Quantity: how many copies you want. Color Printing: Color.
  3. Orientation: Portrait. Size: Letter (8.5" x 11"). Pick the trim size, not the size with bleed; the bleed is already in the file.
  4. Paper Type: Coated, then Satin. Paper Weight: 100lb Text for heft, or 80lb Text to save a little.
  5. Choose Binding: Staple. Pages: set it to —.
  6. Leave the separate cover off. Your cover is page 1 of the file and the back cover is the last page.
  7. Under Choose Price and Shipping Date, pick one. Design Option: Upload Own Artwork, then Add to Cart.
  8. Click Continue. In the Artwork tab, drag the PDF in or click Upload files.
  9. Flip through the Preview and open the PDF Proof. Check nothing important on the cover crosses the trim line. Then fill in Details and Payment.

File: Letter 8.5 × 11 in trim, 0.125 in bleed on every side, page count a multiple of 4. Steps checked against mixam.com on 28 Oct 2026.

  1. Go to mixam.com/magazines and scroll to Create Your Magazines.
  2. Quantity: how many copies you want. Color Printing: Color.
  3. Orientation: Portrait. Size: Letter (8.5" x 11"). The bleed is already in both files.
  4. Paper Type: Coated, then Satin. Paper Weight: 80lb Text, the same as above, because the spine width depends on it.
  5. Choose Binding: Perfect (PUR). Pages: set it to —. That is the inside pages only; the cover is counted separately.
  6. Cover Paper Type: Coated (2-Sided), then Satin. Cover Paper Weight: 100lb Cover. Cover Printing: Color. Inner Cover Printing: No.
  7. Choose Cover Finish: Gloss Lamination or Matte Lamination.
  8. Under Choose Price and Shipping Date, pick one. Design Option: Upload Own Artwork, then Add to Cart.
  9. In the cart, find Spine Width. If it isn't — inch, type Mixam's number here and download the cover again: in
  10. Click Continue. In the Artwork tab, upload both files: the inside pages and the cover.
  11. Flip through the Preview and open the PDF Proof. Check the spine lines up and nothing important on the cover crosses the trim line. Then fill in Details and Payment.

Inside pages: Letter 8.5 × 11 in trim, 0.125 in bleed, a multiple of 4 pages (at least 32). Cover: one spread, back on the left, front on the right, spine in the middle, 0.125 in bleed on every side. The spine is Mixam's calipers for the paper you picked; Mixam's cart has the final word, and can shift the spine up to 0.08 in, so the back, spine and front share one colour.   2. Click UPLOAD A FILE and choose the PDF. You can continue as a guest.
  3. PRINT COLOR: Full Color. PAPER SIZE: 8.5in x 11in.
  4. PAPER TYPE: the default is Laser (32 lb.). A heavier professional white paper feels more like a magazine.
  5. SIDES: change Single-Sided to Double-Sided.
  6. Under binding, choose stapled (coil and comb are also offered).
  7. Set QUANTITY, then Add to Cart. Choose a store for pickup and check out. Most orders are ready the same day or within 24 hours.
